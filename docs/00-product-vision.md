# Product Vision

`reforma-agent` helps owners follow a home renovation when they cannot visit the construction site regularly.

The product centers on trusted site visits. A visitor records notes, photos, audio, documents, issues, decisions, and budget context. Owners receive reviewable information instead of scattered messages.

## Primary Users

- Owners who live away from the renovation site.
- Trusted visitors who document work on site.
- Project helpers who review issues, decisions, documents, and weekly updates.

## MVP Outcomes

- Site visits are documented consistently.
- Photos and audio are stored as private evidence.
- Transcripts and summaries are reviewable.
- Issues and decisions are traceable.
- AI output is always a draft.

## Non-goals

- AI image analysis.
- Autonomous decision making.
- Builder claims or legal assertions.
- Telegram as a core runtime.
- NanoClaw or OpenClaw as core runtime.
