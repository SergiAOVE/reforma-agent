# ADR 0002: No AI Vision in the MVP

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Photos are useful evidence for owners, visitors, and builders. Automatic interpretation of renovation photos can create false confidence and privacy risk.

## Decision

Do not use AI image analysis in the MVP. Photos are stored and referenced as evidence only. Users may add manual notes to photos.

## Consequences

- The MVP avoids unsupported visual claims.
- AI workflows stay text-based.
- Future image analysis would require a new ADR and explicit approval.
